Why charter

Why Charter a Bus for a Corporate Event?

One bus puts the whole group on one schedule. Instead of fifty rideshare receipts and fifty different arrival times, you get one departure, one arrival and one bill.

  • Parking stops being a cost. Hotels and venues charge for every space. A coach needs one loading zone.
  • The agenda holds. The group leaves the hotel together and is seated before the keynote starts.
  • People talk on the way. Teams catch up on the ride to a retreat, and clients ride with their hosts.
  • Nobody drives after the dinner. A licensed professional driver handles the road, which covers your duty of care.
  • Finance sees one number. The quote is written and final, so it can be approved before the event.

FAQ

Corporate Transportation Questions

What event planners and office managers ask us most.

Rather talk it through?+1 (646) 705-0816

Can you run a recurring employee shuttle, not just a one-time event?

Yes. Send us the weekly pattern: pickup points, times and days. That is enough to price a recurring route. We run daily employee shuttles with the same driver for a full quarter, as well as weekly and event-season routes.

Do your vehicles have Wi-Fi for conference attendees?

It depends on the vehicle assigned in your city. If your group needs Wi-Fi, say so in your quote request and we will confirm in writing whether the assigned vehicle has it before you book.

How far in advance should we book conference transportation?

Book as soon as your dates and headcount are firm. Big conferences and the spring and fall event seasons fill vehicles fast, so several weeks ahead is safest. Short-notice requests are welcome too. Written quotes go out the same day, and we take pickups at any hour.

Can you handle transportation for a multi-day event?

Yes. Send the schedule for each day, from hotel departures and sessions to dinners and the final airport run. We price the whole event as one booking with one quote, and driver overnight stays and late pickups are included in that price.

Do you provide invoices for business bookings?

Yes. You get a written quote and itinerary you can forward for internal approval. Share your billing needs, such as a PO number or invoice details, when you request a quote and we will confirm them before booking.

Can we mix vehicle types for one corporate event?

Yes. A large group might need a charter bus for the main group, a minibus for staff and a Sprinter van for a venue a bus cannot reach. We plan the mix in your quote instead of sending whatever is free that day.

Do you use school buses for corporate trips?

Yes, for short runs where cost matters more than comfort, like a parking-lot shuttle or a quick hotel-to-airport transfer. For longer trips, a charter bus with reclining seats and luggage space is the better choice, and we will tell you which one fits.

What is included in a corporate charter quote?

The vehicle, the driver, gratuity, tolls and taxes. There are no fuel surcharges or driver fees added later, so the price you approve is the price you pay.
