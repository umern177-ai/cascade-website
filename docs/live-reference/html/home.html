Our Heritage

Built on Punctuality, Honesty, and Hard Work

Cascade Charter Bus started with just one vehicle, a small garage, and a lot of hard work. There was no big investment and no fleet waiting in the wings. We started with one core belief: that punctuality and honesty are the fundamental principles of every journey we take.

We didn’t become a nationwide company overnight. For more than 15 years, we’ve grown little by little—one trip, one customer, one referral at a time. Every early pickup and late drop-off taught us something we carried into the next trip. That is why reliability is not something that we just advertise; it is something we built the company around.

What started as a small local operation has grown into a premier charter bus service serving groups across the country. We’ve helped thousands of people reach their destination safely—whether that’s guests attending a wedding, students going on a field trip, teams heading to championship games, coworkers traveling for corporate events, families gathering for reunions, or friends setting off together.

Guided by one unwavering principle: get people to their destinations, safely and on time, every single trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get clear answers on charter bus rentals, pricing transparency, and booking details.

Pricing depends on your overall group size, the vehicle type required, trip distance, and seasonal demand. A sprinter van or school bus typically offers a lower rate than a full-size motorcoach. Contact us directly for a fast, free quote custom-tailored to your exact itinerary.

For major events like weddings or travel during peak seasonal months, we advise reserving your bus 4 to 8 weeks in advance. For smaller local trips or off-peak dates, 1 to 2 weeks notice is generally sufficient to secure your preferred vehicle.

It comes down to passenger headcount and luggage space. As a rule of thumb: Sprinter vans accommodate 10–14 people, minibuses seat 20–30, and full-size motorcoaches comfortably fit up to 56 passengers. Our logistics team will readily guide you to the right vehicle size.

Absolutely! A school bus is usually the most economical per-seat choice available. It serves as an ultra-practical option for school trips, and an increasing number of wedding planners choose them for guest shuttles when budget efficiency takes priority over luxury amenities.

Our charter bus rental rates are fully transparent and all-inclusive—meaning zero surprise fees. Your quoted price covers the vehicle rental, a professional driver, tolls, taxes, and driver gratuity. You receive one clear, comprehensive price for your entire trip.

To ensure complete peace of mind, we always encourage asking: Is the quoted price fully all-inclusive? Are there any hidden fees or charges? How are extra services during the trip handled? Is driver tip included upfront? Do you offer active dispatch support? What is the contingency plan if something unexpected happens to the bus? Do the coaches feature onboard restrooms?
