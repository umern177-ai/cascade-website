Know before you book

Charter Bus Rental in Shelton, CT for Offices and Events

Shelton sits on Route 8, a few miles north of the Merritt Parkway, and that parkway is the first thing to know about moving a group here. Connecticut bans buses from it. Any trip toward Stamford or New York City runs on I-95 instead, and that changes the drive time.

Cascade Charter Bus arranges charter buses, minibuses and Sprinter vans in Shelton for office shuttles, weddings, school outings and group days in New York — always on a route a bus can legally drive.

School and Field Trips

1

Jones Family Farms

White Hills, Shelton · Farm & winery

This 400-acre farm in Shelton's White Hills has been in the same family since 1848. Groups come for strawberries and blueberries in summer, pumpkins in the fall and cut-your-own Christmas trees in December. There is a winery on site for adult groups.

Bus drop-off tip: October and December weekends are very busy and the farm roads are narrow. Call the farm ahead to book a group visit and ask where a bus should unload.

2

Indian Well State Park

Lake Housatonic · State park

Indian Well is a wooded state park on Lake Housatonic with a 15-foot waterfall, picnic groves and a swimming beach with lifeguards in summer. It is a popular spot for camp groups and company picnics.

Bus drop-off tip: The lot fills on hot summer weekends and the park closes its gate when it does. Arrive before 10 a.m., and check bus parking and group fees with Connecticut State Parks before you book.

3

Sports Center of Connecticut

River Road · Family entertainment

This family entertainment centre on River Road has double-decker ice rinks, mini golf, bowling, laser tag and a driving range. Youth hockey teams, birthday groups and school reward trips all end up here, often in winter when outdoor options are few.

Bus drop-off tip: The centre has a large lot right off Route 110. The bus unloads at the main entrance, and the driver parks on site until your group is ready to leave.

4

Veterans Memorial Park and the Riverwalk

Downtown Shelton · Riverfront

Downtown Shelton's riverfront park hosts summer concerts, food trucks and the city's fireworks, with a Saturday farmers' market next door in spring and summer. The old factory buildings around it now hold restaurants, which makes it an easy dinner stop for a group.

Bus drop-off tip: Streets near the river are tight and some close on event nights. We set a drop point a block or two back and agree a pickup time before the crowd leaves.

5

New York City

~65 miles · Broadway, museums, holiday trips

Shelton groups head to Manhattan for Broadway shows, museum days and holiday trips. The drive is roughly 65 miles. Because buses are banned from the Merritt Parkway, the trip runs on I-95, which is slower in rush hour than a car's map suggests.

Bus drop-off tip: Leave before 7 a.m. or after 9:30 a.m. to miss the worst traffic. Buses in Manhattan may only stop at marked bus zones, so we pick the drop-off nearest your first stop.
